About Ghulam Ali Amiri

Ghulam Ali Amiri is currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Information Technology at King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi (KMUTT) in Thailand. He has more than ten years of experience in teaching, academic leadership, and research, including roles as an Assistant Professor, Head of Department, and Dean of the Faculty of Computer Science at Ghazni University in Afghanistan. He earned his Master's degree in Computer Science from Savitribai Phule Pune University in India and his Bachelor's degree in Information Technology from Kabul University in Afghanistan. His research focuses on Information Systems, Digital Transformation, Digital Humanism, and Generative Artificial Intelligence, especially digital transformation maturity models and digital capabilities in higher education.
